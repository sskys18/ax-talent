# Investor Questions: NVIDIA (NVDA)

Use these questions to build your own judgment. They are prompts, not answers.

## Buffett Lens

### moat
- Can you explain in one sentence why competitors cannot easily copy this business?
- Has the margin been stable across several years, not just the latest one?

### cash flow
- Where does the cash actually come from, and is it recurring?
- Is free cash flow growing or shrinking over recent years?

### margin of safety
- What has to go right for the company to justify today's price?
- How far could the price fall if growth slows, and could you tolerate that?

### quality
- Would this business survive a bad year in its industry without distress?

## Peter Lynch Lens

### understandable story
- Can you explain this company to a friend in about 30 seconds?
- Do you personally use or understand its products?

### growth
- Is the growth coming from new customers, price increases, or one-time effects?
- Could this growth rate realistically hold over your 3 years horizon?

### valuation versus growth
- If the growth rate halved, would the valuation still make sense to you?

## Dalio Lens

### macro cycle
- In a recession, would people spend more or less on this product?

### diversification
- What percent of your total portfolio would this position be?
- Do you already own similar companies in Semiconductors?

### interest rate sensitivity
- If interest rates rose sharply, how might this stock be affected?

## Momentum Lens

### trend
- Are you reacting to the recent price move rather than to the business itself?

### price action
- Could you stay calm if this dropped 30% in a single month?

### market reaction
- Do you have a plan for what you would do on a large down day?

## Dividend Lens

### dividend stability
- Are you here for income, growth, or both, and does this stock match that?

### cash flow
- Where does the cash actually come from, and is it recurring?
- Is free cash flow growing or shrinking over recent years?

### defensive quality
- Do you need this position to be stable, and does the data support that expectation?

## Beginner Safety Check

- Can you explain what this company does without using jargon?
- Would you be comfortable if this position fell 30% during your 3 years horizon?
- Is this money you will not need during your 3 years horizon?
- Would this position be a reasonable share of your portfolio, not an oversized bet?
- Are you deciding from data and process, not from fear of missing out?
- As a self-described beginner investor with medium risk tolerance, does this fit your plan?
